Context & reflection

Explore Argentina's History

Understanding Argentina requires looking at political, economic and social changes together.

Historical periods

Six broad chapters

1816–1852

Independence & conflict

Independence was followed by struggles over political organization, federalism and the relationship between Buenos Aires and the provinces.

1853–1916

Nation building

Constitutional organization, economic expansion, immigration and territorial consolidation transformed the country.

1916–1943

Mass politics

Electoral reforms expanded political participation, while Argentina experienced social and economic changes during the first half of the twentieth century.

1943–1976

Peronism & instability

Peronism became a central force in Argentine politics, alongside periods of military intervention and democratic government.

1976–1983

Dictatorship & Malvinas

Military rule, state terrorism and the 1982 Falklands/Malvinas War profoundly affected Argentine society and politics.

1983–2026

Democratic Argentina

Democratic continuity was accompanied by constitutional reform, economic crises, social changes and major shifts between political movements.
