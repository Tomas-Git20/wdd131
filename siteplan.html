Argentina Through Time

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Argentina Through Time

The name was chosen because it clearly communicates the purpose of the website: exploring the history of Argentina from its independence in 1816 to the present day through an organized historical timeline.

Site Purpose

This website provides an educational overview of Argentina's history, covering the country's independence, nation building, political developments, economic transformations, cultural milestones, and modern events. Visitors will be able to explore major historical periods, important figures, and key events through an accessible, interactive, and responsive website.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • What were the most important events after Argentina became independent in 1816?
  • How did Peronism influence Argentina's political and social development?
  • Which historical figures had the greatest impact on Argentina?
