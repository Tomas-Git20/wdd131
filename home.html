Key turning points

Five moments to remember

1816

Independence

The Congress of Tucumán formally declared independence from Spanish rule and from any other foreign domination.

1853

Constitution

The Constitution established a federal constitutional framework for the emerging nation.

1946

Peronism

Juan Domingo Perón became president and a major political movement emerged around labor, social rights and state intervention.

1983

Democracy returns

Raúl Alfonsín took office after democratic elections, beginning the country's longest continuous democratic period.

2001

Economic crisis

A severe economic, political and social crisis produced a dramatic change of government and reshaped Argentine politics.

2023

A new political direction

Javier Milei was elected president, beginning a new period centered on economic liberalization and major institutional and economic reforms.
